# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in **The Playwright Engineering Handbook** community a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

Our goal is to foster an environment where engineers, testers, developers, architects, students, and contributors can collaborate, learn, and share knowledge in a professional manner regardless of:

* Experience level
* Job title
* Education
* Nationality
* Ethnicity
* Gender identity
* Sexual orientation
* Disability
* Age
* Religion
* Personal background

We believe that respectful collaboration leads to better engineering and stronger communities.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and professional.
* Welcoming constructive discussions.
* Providing helpful feedback.
* Sharing knowledge openly.
* Respecting different technical opinions.
* Focusing on solving problems rather than assigning blame.
* Supporting new contributors.
* Giving credit where it is due.
* Using inclusive and considerate language.
* Demonstrating patience when helping others.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable:

* Harassment of any kind.
* Personal attacks or insults.
* Discrimination.
* Hate speech.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Trolling.
* Deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Offensive comments based on personal characteristics.
* Repeatedly ignoring project guidelines.
* Spam or promotional content unrelated to the project.

Professional disagreements are welcome; disrespectful behavior is not.

---

# Engineering Discussions

Healthy technical debate is encouraged.

When discussing ideas:

* Critique the solution, not the person.
* Support opinions with technical reasoning.
* Assume positive intent.
* Remain open to learning.
* Respect maintainers' final decisions.

The objective is to improve the project—not to win arguments.

---

# Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Clarifying project expectations.
* Reviewing contributions fairly.
* Encouraging respectful collaboration.
* Addressing inappropriate behavior promptly.
* Taking corrective action when necessary.

Maintainers reserve the right to edit, remove, reject, or close comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

# Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity of the violation, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

1. Friendly reminder.
2. Private warning.
3. Temporary restriction from project discussions.
4. Temporary suspension.
5. Permanent ban from the project.

The chosen action will depend on the nature and severity of the incident.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions related to this repository, including:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Code Reviews
* Documentation
* Community contributions
* Project communication channels

It also applies when individuals officially represent this project in public spaces.

---

# Our Philosophy

The mission of **The Playwright Engineering Handbook** is to promote high-quality software engineering through practical education, enterprise architecture, and collaborative learning.

We believe that great software is built by communities that value:

* Professionalism
* Respect
* Curiosity
* Continuous learning
* Knowledge sharing
* Engineering excellence

Every contributor helps strengthen that mission.
